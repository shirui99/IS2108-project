Stock advice

Review packs to replenish or check for promotion. Each product and pack size has its own stock count. Staff decide what action to take.

Suggested replenishment is the target minus sellable stock, with a minimum of zero. An unset target gives no suggestion. Inactive products are labelled for review before replenishing.

Recorded available counts include every pack with warehouse status available. Sellable counts require an active product and a recorded date on or after today plus its shelf-life cutoff days, or no recorded date. Below-cutoff counts show dated available packs excluded by that rule. No stock status changes automatically. Received batches may record Use by, Best before or Expiry dates; legacy date types may be unknown. The same cutoff rule applies regardless of printed date type.
